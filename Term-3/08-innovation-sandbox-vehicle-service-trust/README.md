## The question / brief
_The original brief was not in my files; this is reconstructed from the submission._

Group Innovation Sandbox project carried through every design-thinking stage - Empathise, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test and Scale - on one problem (here: trust in vehicle servicing), submitted as phase reports and a final presentation.


## What I did
Full design-thinking cycle on why urban Indian car owners distrust authorised service centres: empathise, sharp problem statement, ideation with six triggers and assumption busting, prototype (live service-status link and proactive cost updates), test and scale plan.
