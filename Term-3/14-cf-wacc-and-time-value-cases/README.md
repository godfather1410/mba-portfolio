# Domino's Pizza WACC + Time-Value-of-Money Cases

**Course:** Corporate Finance  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 3 (Aug-Sep 2026)  
**Type:** Group project (team members are credited inside the submission)  
**Skills:** WACC, CAPM, time value of money

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

Domino's Pizza Enterprises: what is the cost of capital and why does it matter; how to estimate cost of debt and cost of equity; book-value and market-value WACC; WACC at an 18% debt-to-equity ratio and under low/high/actual weights; how WACC is used for future projects (assumptions stated in Excel).

Time value of money cases: ESOP buyback (cash now vs later), child education fund, home-loan prepayment, lump sum vs SIP under three return scenarios.


## What I did
Weighted average cost of capital for Domino's Pizza Enterprises (cost of equity, cost of debt, capital-structure weights from exhibits), and a workbook of applied time-value cases (ESOP buyback, child education fund, home-loan prepayment, lump sum vs SIP).
