# Industry benchmarks, mid-tier multi-brand retail

Reference values for benchmarking. Update as the sector moves; instructions stay unchanged.

| Metric | Industry norm | Source of norm |
|---|---|---|
| E-commerce share of net revenue | 34% | stated in the source document as the industry average |
| Inventory turnover | 4.0x to 5.0x | sector range for mid-tier apparel and home goods |
| Enclosed-mall foot traffic trend | declining mid-single digits annually | multi-year sector shift |
| Digital share of marketing and capex budget | 25% to 35% | competitor disclosures in the source document |

## How to use these

- Report the gap in percentage points, not as a judgement word.
- A gap wider than 15 pp on channel mix is a structural position, not a tactical one, and belongs
  in the threat ranking rather than the observations list.
- Where the document supplies its own industry comparator, prefer it over the values here and say
  which you used.

## Channel definitions
- **Enclosed mall:** anchored indoor centres. Structural decline in this format.
- **Open-air / strip:** convenience-led formats. Holding or growing.
- **Digital:** owned e-commerce plus marketplace.
