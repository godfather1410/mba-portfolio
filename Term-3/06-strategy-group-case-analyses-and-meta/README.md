## The question / brief
_The original brief was not in my files; this is reconstructed from the submission._

Group case analyses for Strategic Management sessions (Starbucks, IBM, Honda, Satyam, 'Who is a Professional?', ICF Vande Bharat) presented as short decks.

Term project: pick an industry and a company (Technology / Meta Platforms) and assess (1) the structure of the industry, (2) the company's competitive position and (3) its business strength, using the course frameworks (industry life cycle, Five Forces, BCG / GE-McKinsey matrices).


## What I did
Group case decks: Starbucks (Howard Schultz), IBM dynamic capabilities, Honda's US market entry, Satyam governance failure, 'Who is a Professional?' and ICF's Vande Bharat launch; plus the term project on Meta Platforms: industry structure, competitive position and business strength.
