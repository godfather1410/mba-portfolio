# Innovation Sandbox: Post-Mortem, Discovery Interviews & Cab-Availability Project

**Course:** Innovation Sandbox  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 3 (Aug-Sep 2026)  
**Type:** Individual  
**Skills:** Customer discovery, design thinking, innovation frameworks

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

Innovation Sandbox individual work: (1) failure post-mortem of a start-up (Hike) - wrong problem, customer or timing?; (2) talk to 3 real people about a problem, asking about past behaviour, not hypothetical use; (3) map a company's innovations by type, mode and ocean; (4) final individual assignment: pick a recurring problem of someone close to you and go through Empathise, Define, Size the opportunity, Ideate (with an evaluation matrix), Prototype and Test.


## What I did
Hike Messenger failure post-mortem (wrong problem / customer / timing), three customer-discovery interviews on EV range and charging in Delhi-NCR, innovation mapping for The Souled Store (type, mode, ocean), and a design-thinking project on peak-hour cab availability for office commuters in Noida.
