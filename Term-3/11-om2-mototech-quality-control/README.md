# MotoTech Manufacturing: Process Control & Capability

**Course:** Operations Management II  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 3 (Aug-Sep 2026)  
**Type:** Individual  
**Skills:** Statistical process control, process capability

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

MotoTech case: what triggered the request for help and why it matters; which process stage, characteristics and spec limits are involved; the sampling plan; 'in control' vs 'capable'; why read the R chart before the X-bar chart.

Part A: should MotoTech wait for the customer to relax the spec? Part B: build X-bar and R charts from 72 pre-improvement subgroups, assess capability, then evaluate the post-improvement process and recommend actions.


## What I did
Case on a supplier facing a drastically tightened customer specification: control charts, process capability against the new spec, root causes and an improvement plan, with Excel workings.
