## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

Liz Motor Corp (Ivey case): qualitative assessment - is Liz well positioned? What hurdle rate should the project use?

Base model: project cash flows 2023-2035; compute NPV, payback, IRR and PI. Sensitivity analysis on the listed uncertainties (higher cost of debt, green bond, EU carbon border tax upside, sale of the technology, faster-than-expected value, hydrogen breakthrough risk).


## What I did
Is an $800M EV/ESG project worth it? Qualitative positioning, hurdle-rate choice, NPV/IRR on base-case cash flows and scenario analysis, in Excel with a written answer set.
