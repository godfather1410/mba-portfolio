# Strategy Cases with AI as Sparring Partner (6 cases)

**Course:** Strategic Management  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 3 (Aug-Sep 2026)  
**Type:** Individual  
**Skills:** Strategic analysis, frameworks, critical use of AI

## The question / brief
_The original brief was not in my files; this is reconstructed from the submission._

Individual AI-augmented strategy cases. For each case, write your own summary and answers first, then use AI (with anonymised company names, no outside facts) as a sparring partner that finds gaps and argues against you, and submit the full prompt trail plus your final answer. Questions included:

- Allentown Materials: is the conflict in the Electronic Products Division serious, and what should Don Rogers do?
- Thyrocare: evaluate the disruptive model and its sustainability.
- Stitch Fix: role of gender in funding; what it took from retail, stylists and subscription boxes; blue-ocean logic vs an AI-powered rival.
- Palantir: a) explain the strategy and value-creation model; b) how has it disrupted the competition?
- Reliance: a) explain the strategy; b) define its core competency and contrast it with another firm's.
- Schindler India: what should the subsidiary head tell the visiting vice chairman and do in the next 90 days?

## What I did
Six individual case analyses where AI was used as a critic, not an author: anonymised prompts, AI pushback and my final recommendation for Allentown Materials (organisational conflict), Thyrocare (disruptive business model), Stitch Fix (blue ocean + AI), Palantir (decisions aligned with values), Reliance Industries (execution excellence) and Schindler India (international market entry).
