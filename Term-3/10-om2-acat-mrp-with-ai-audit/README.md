# A-CAT Corp: Multi-Level MRP + AI Audit

**Course:** Operations Management II  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 3 (Aug-Sep 2026)  
**Type:** Individual  
**Skills:** MRP, lot sizing, AI verification

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

HW2: 1. Diagnose Menon's production-planning problem at A-CAT Corp (150-200 words). 2. Build the MRP matrix for every item in the product structure. 3. Interpret: effect of Week-1 scheduled receipts, which item builds excess inventory, what to raise before accepting the plan.

AI-augmented audit: have AI build the same MRP, record deviations, refine the prompt until it matches; then probe whether the lot sizes are fixed inputs or a reviewable policy and build a revised lot-sizing scenario from setup, unit and carrying costs.


## What I did
8-week material requirements plan for a three-level bill of materials (P0110 family) with lead times and on-hand stock, then an AI-augmented audit: checking an AI-generated MRP against mine, refining the prompt, and questioning whether fixed lot sizes should be revisited using the cost data.
