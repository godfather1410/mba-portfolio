# Skoda Kushaq: Integrated Operations Plan

**Course:** Operations Management II  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 3 (Aug-Sep 2026)  
**Type:** Group project (team members are credited inside the submission)  
**Skills:** Aggregate planning, MPS, MRP, inventory policy

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

Term project: act as an OM consulting group for a company of your choice and build a business case solving:

Scenario 1 - Aggregate Production Plan (>= 6 periods, hybrid level/chase strategy) and detailed MRP charts; further scenarios on inventory policy for a key part and related operational decisions, all with stated assumptions. Chosen product: Skoda Kushaq.


## What I did
Term project acting as Skoda's internal OM team: 8-month hybrid aggregate production plan for 12,500 units, master production schedule, 4-level MRP (vehicle, engine/chassis/body, parts) and an inventory policy for a key purchased part, with all workings in Excel.
