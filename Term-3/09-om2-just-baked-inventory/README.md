# Just Baked: Inventory Management for Perishables

**Course:** Operations Management II

**Type:** Individual  
**Skills:** Newsvendor model, inventory policy

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

Just Baked case template: 1. Background. 2. Key inventory challenges. 3. Which inventory model fits cupcakes, cake mix and eggs, and why?

4. Analysis per item - why is the cupcake decision a newsvendor problem; what service level for cake mix; why eggs are not single-period; why franchisees cannot set their own cupcake orders. 5. Managerial recommendation.


## What I did
Inventory policy for a cupcake chain where unsold stock is thrown out daily: newsvendor model (cost of under/over-stocking, critical ratio), and periodic vs continuous review policies for ingredients.
