# Credit-Card Customer Descriptive Statistics

**Course:** Statistical Methods for Decision Making (SMDM-1)  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 1 (May-Jul 2026)  
**Type:** Individual  
**Skills:** Descriptive statistics, data visualisation, Excel pivots

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

You are an analyst at a bank with a credit-card customer dataset. Using statistics, answer:

1-3. Build suitable visual, tabular and numerical summaries and comment on them.

4. Mean income and its first, second and third quartiles.

5. Standard deviation and IQR of income - are they very different, and what does that tell you?

6. Boxplot of card balance and what it shows.

7. Classify customers as 'low' (1-3 cards) or 'high' (>3 cards) and build a contingency table by region; interpret.

Report max 3 pages, plus Excel/code and AI prompts used.


## What I did
Acting as a bank analyst on a 400-customer credit-card dataset (income, limit, rating, cards, age, education, region): mean, quartiles, IQR and standard deviation, outlier checks, pivot summaries by region and charts, interpreted for managerial decisions.
