Excel workshop practice sets (Sessions 1-5):

- Financial functions: loan payments with PMT (monthly/quarterly, start vs end of period), balloon payments, principal and interest split (PPMT/IPMT), and how much to save each month to reach an investment goal (FV/PMT).
- Lookup functions: join a product master with a supplier master for 'ZenithMart' and answer pricing/margin/lead-time questions.
- Bank analysis workspace: answer metric questions on a loan portfolio (by branch, region, product, borrower segment).
- Descriptive statistics on an insurance dataset (age, BMI, smoker, region, charges).

## What I did
Practice cases on Excel financial functions (PMT, PPMT, IPMT, FV) covering loans, balloon payments, payment timing and investment goals; lookup-function exercises on a retail product/supplier master (ZenithMart); a loan-portfolio analysis workspace for a fictional bank (IndiaFirst Commercial Bank); and descriptive statistics on an insurance dataset.
