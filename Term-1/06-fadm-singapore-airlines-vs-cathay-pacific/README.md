# Singapore Airlines vs Cathay Pacific: Financial Analysis

**Course:** Financial Accounting for Decision Making (FADM)  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 1 (May-Jul 2026)  
**Type:** Group project (team members are credited inside the submission)  
**Skills:** Financial statement analysis, ratio analysis, Excel modelling

## The question / brief
_The original brief was not in my files; this is reconstructed from the submission._

FADM group project: pick a company and its closest competitor and analyse them from their published financial statements.

Part I: company and competitor overview, business segments and multi-year revenue/expense trends.

Further parts: acquisitions, investments and financing activity, and an executive workbook comparing key metrics and ratios.

Companies chosen: Singapore Airlines and Cathay Pacific.


## What I did
Comparative financial-statement analysis of two premium Asian airlines: company and competitor overview, business segments, five-year revenue and expense trends (converted to INR), acquisitions and financing (incl. the Air India-Vistara merger), and an executive Excel workbook comparing headline metrics and ratios.
