## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

Case: 'RBI Cuts, Fed Holds: Who Wins and Who Bleeds?' (Aug 2026) - RBI cuts repo 25 bps to 5.00%, the Fed holds at 3.25-3.75%, the rupee weakens, FII outflows continue and commodity prices stay high. You are a macro analyst at your assigned firm (Hero MotoCorp) preparing a rapid note for the board.

- Q1. Write the primary prompt you used to analyse how this macro environment affects the firm.
- Q2. Summarise the AI's response in your own words (150-200 words).
- Q3. Find one real news article or data release from the last 60 days relevant to the firm, its sector or a key macro variable.
- Q4. Identify the most important analytical gap in the AI's response (150-200 words).
- Q5. Write a 100-150 word recommendation to the CFO given the rate cut, rupee depreciation and FII outflows.
- Q6. Describe a moment where the AI's output was genuinely useful.
- Q7. Describe a moment where you disagreed with, corrected or discarded the AI's output, and how you detected the error.

## What I did
Board-ready assessment of how an RBI repo cut, a Fed hold, rupee depreciation, FII outflows and high commodity prices transmit to Hero MotoCorp's input costs, EV investment and two-wheeler demand. Includes the verbatim AI prompt, an own-words summary and a critique of the AI output.
