# Ideathon: AI-Driven Career Upskilling Product Concept

**Course:** Ideathon  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 1 (May-Jul 2026)  
**Type:** Group project (team members are credited inside the submission)  
**Skills:** Product thinking, personas, concept design

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

Ideathon Case 3 (EdTech): LearnEdge, an online learning platform for working professionals, has strong enrolments but low completion and repeat purchase.

Problem: what should LearnEdge build to improve engagement, completion and career outcomes? Constraint: you cannot add more content or lower prices.

Deliverables: customer insight, product concept, user journey, value proposition, business model, GTM, operational feasibility, financial logic, success metrics, plus a tangible prototype or simulation.


## What I did
Product concept for a learner-retention problem: customer insights and personas, an AI onboarding diagnostic that builds personalised sprint plans, peer pods with leaderboards, and career-linked levels.
