# Vietnam Macroeconomic Country Report

**Course:** Managerial Economics (Macro)  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 1 (May-Jul 2026)  
**Type:** Group project (team members are credited inside the submission)  
**Skills:** Macroeconomics, policy analysis, presentation

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

Each group gets a country (Vietnam). Build a slide deck (max 12 slides) that covers:

(i) A socio-political-economic overview of the country.

(ii) Macro performance FY2022-25 plus forecasts for 2026-27: growth, inflation, unemployment, industrial production, balance of payments, public finances.

(iii) Three distinct macroeconomic difficulties (at least two directly macroeconomic).

(iv) Remedial policy measures for each, written as a policy analyst.


## What I did
Slide deck on Vietnam: socio-political-economic overview, macro performance FY2022-25 with 2026-27 forecasts (growth, inflation, unemployment, trade), three macroeconomic difficulties and remedial policy measures, with an AI-prompt appendix. Sources: World Bank, IMF, OECD, GSO Vietnam.
