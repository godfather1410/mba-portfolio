# Business Cost Structure Field Study

**Course:** Managerial Economics (Micro)  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 1 (May-Jul 2026)  
**Type:** Group project (team members are credited inside the submission)  
**Skills:** Cost curves, field research

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

Microeconomics group assignment: understand business costs through a real local business.

1. Watch the two videos on cost curves. 2. Photograph the business and ask an AI tool to estimate its cost structure (paste prompt + answer).

3. Interview the owner: list every cost, which stay fixed vs rise with customers, peak vs off-peak output, what happens at peak.

4. Talk to 2-3 customers. 5. Classify every cost as fixed or variable, build a cost table across output levels and plot AFC, AVC, ATC and MC; compare with the AI estimate.


## What I did
Field study of a local transport business: AI-estimated cost structure vs owner interview, fixed vs variable costs, and AFC/AVC/ATC/MC curves against output.
