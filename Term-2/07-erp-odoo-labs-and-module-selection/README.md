## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

(a) Odoo Business Challenge on the SmartBrew Coffee case: e.g. run recruitment and onboarding end-to-end, launch a new coffee-capsule product (product, BOM, purchase, manufacture, sell, invoice, payment) - evidenced with screenshots.

(b) ERP Simulation Lab workbook (SmartBrew Coffee Pvt Ltd): configure and run a company in Odoo across sales, procurement, manufacturing, warehouse and finance.

(c) Short in-class assignment: rank the five ERP modules most critical for a manufacturer and justify.


## What I did
Screenshot evidence of configuring and running transactions in Odoo ERP (three activities) and the SmartBrew ERP simulation lab, plus a short assignment ranking the five ERP modules that matter most for a multi-plant industrial manufacturer.
