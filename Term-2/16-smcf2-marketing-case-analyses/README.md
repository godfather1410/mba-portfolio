# Marketing Case Analyses: ACK, Asian Paints, London 2012, Planet Milk

**Course:** Strategic Marketing & Customer Focus (SMCF-2)  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 2 (Jul-Aug 2026)  
**Type:** Group project (team members are credited inside the submission)  
**Skills:** Marketing strategy, pricing, channel design

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

Marketing case submissions, each with three bold sections:

a) Case Summary (max 250 words, own words); b) Identification of Issues - the central marketing problem with evidence from the case and relevant concepts; c) Proposed Solution - clear, specific and justified.

Cases: Amar Chitra Katha, Asian Paints BHPS, London 2012 Olympics ticketing, Planet Milk.


## What I did
Four group case write-ups: Amar Chitra Katha's quest for growth in a digital world, Asian Paints' Beautiful Homes Painting Service, London 2012 Olympics ticket pricing, and Planet Milk's route-to-market (with workings). Each covers case summary, core problem, options and recommendation.
