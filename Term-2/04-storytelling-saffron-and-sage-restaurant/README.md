# Saffron & Sage: 5-Star Reviews, Empty Tables

**Course:** Storytelling with Data  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 2 (Jul-Aug 2026)  
**Type:** Group project (team members are credited inside the submission)  
**Skills:** Data storytelling, synthetic data design, executive presentation

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

Group assignment: craft a story of 10 minutes max that convinces a business audience to act on insights from self-generated (synthetic) data.

Problem statement template: 'How can <organisation/stakeholder> achieve ...?'. Include business context, 1-2 analytical questions, a synthetic dataset (> 8 columns, >= 5,000 rows) with justified dimensions, a visual story (5-6 slides or Tableau dashboards) using Gestalt / storytelling frameworks, a hook, implications, and a recommendation with a call to action.


## What I did
Final group presentation for the COO of a fictional 12-outlet restaurant chain where Google ratings rose (4.52 to 4.68) while footfall fell 23%. The deck interrogates the data (measurement, external factors, new vs returning guests, what ratings really measure) and recommends where to put a Rs 1.2 crore turnaround budget.
