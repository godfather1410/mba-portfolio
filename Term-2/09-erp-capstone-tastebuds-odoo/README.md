# ERP Consulting Capstone: TasteBuds Restaurant Chain on Odoo

**Course:** ERP & Industry 4.0  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 2 (Jul-Aug 2026)  
**Type:** Group project (team members are credited inside the submission)  
**Skills:** Odoo ERP, BPMN, consulting deliverables

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

Group capstone 'ERP Consulting Challenge': act as an ERP consulting firm implementing Odoo for an assigned client (TasteBuds restaurant chain).

Phase 1 company profile; Phase 2 three BPMN models (Order-to-Cash, Procure-to-Pay, and one industry-specific process); then design the ERP solution, configure Odoo, execute end-to-end transactions, show information flow across modules and recommend Industry 4.0 / AI initiatives.


## What I did
ERP consulting engagement for a multi-branch restaurant chain: company profile, Odoo solution design (POS, inventory, purchase, accounting, HR) on a multi-company architecture, BPMN models for Order-to-Cash, Procure-to-Pay and Ticket-to-Table, a module setup guide and the client presentation.
