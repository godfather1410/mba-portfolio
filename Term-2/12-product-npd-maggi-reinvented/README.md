# Maggi Reinvented: Kotler's 8-Stage NPD Mapping

**Course:** Product Management  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 2 (Jul-Aug 2026)  
**Type:** Individual  
**Skills:** New product development, product strategy

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

Take-home case 'MAGGI Reinvented: New Product Development at Nestle India'. You are the category team evaluating a proposed MAGGI Millet Fusion Cup Noodles.

- Q1. Using Kotler's eight-stage NPD framework, map each stage to a specific MAGGI launch or company action in the case and explain why it fits.
- Q2. Calculate the proposed product's operating profit from the case exhibits.
- Q3. Using the Q2 result and the strategic evidence in the case, recommend one of the given options for the product and justify the choice.

## What I did
Mapped Nestle India's Maggi portfolio expansion (millets, Korean flavours, rural price points, quick-commerce) onto Kotler's eight-stage new product development process, stage by stage, with critique.
