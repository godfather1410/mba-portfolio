## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

You are a Business Analyst at ABC Consulting. A client wants to automate a business process in ERP; first it must be documented in BPMN 2.0 (Bizagi).

Choose one process (chosen: Manufacturing - Plan-to-Produce), analyse it, identify activities, participants, decision points and business rules, and prepare a professional BPMN diagram plus documentation for client management approval.


## What I did
Business-analyst style documentation of a make-to-order Plan-to-Produce process for a luxury automaker ('Premium Auto Ltd.'): BPMN diagram with swimlanes, business rules, process table and report.
