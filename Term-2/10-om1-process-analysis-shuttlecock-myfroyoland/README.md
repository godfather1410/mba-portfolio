# Process Analysis: Shuttlecock Production & Myfroyoland

**Course:** Operations Management I  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 2 (Jul-Aug 2026)  
**Type:** Individual  
**Skills:** Process analysis, capacity & bottlenecks, AI prompting

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

(a) Process mapping with AI: after drawing your own map of an assigned video (handmade shuttlecock production), use NotebookLM with a fixed prompt to extract every step (actor, inputs, outputs, stated figures, sequential/parallel) and Gemini Canvas to build the map; then reflect on the differences.

(b) Mid-term take-home case 'myfroyoland: Weighing the Weekend Rush': draw the process flow, compute the capacity of each stage, find the bottleneck that makes customers walk out on Saturday evenings, and recommend what the store manager should change before the summer rush.


## What I did
(1) AI-assisted extraction of a process map from a video of handmade shuttlecock production: steps, resources, run times, buffers, sequential vs parallel flow, with a strict 'only what is stated' prompt. (2) Mid-term case Myfroyoland: process flow diagram, capacity at each stage, bottleneck identification (single weigh-and-pay counter, 72 customers/hour) and improvement options. The 108 MB process-map PDF is excluded.
