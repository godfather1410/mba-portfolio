# AI Agent Skills for Statistical Inference (10-session series)

**Course:** Statistical Methods for Decision Making (SMDM-2)  
**Programme:** PGPM 2026-27, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Term 2 (Jul-Aug 2026)  
**Type:** Individual  
**Skills:** Hypothesis testing, ANOVA, regression, agentic AI skill design

## The question / brief
_Summarised from the course brief._

SMDM-2 runs as ten sessions; in each, students build an AI-agent 'skill' in Google Antigravity (logic + formulas), give it business data through a prompt, and make the managerial decision themselves ('the AI computes; you decide').

Session topics: S1 sampling and point estimates, S2 confidence intervals, S3 hypothesis framing, S4 Type II error and power, S5 critical-value vs p-value rules, S6 two-population t-tests, S7 ANOVA with Fisher's LSD, S8 simple regression, S9 multiple regression with VIF, S10 regression diagnostics (Humana case).

Session 6 case ('Ten Variables, Six Hypotheses, One Turnaround Plan', Nandan Mart): turn a messy problem into six testable hypotheses, pick the right t-test and tail for each, and combine the results into one plan. My submission re-verified the published solution.


## What I did
Built and applied a library of AI-agent skills in Google Antigravity, one per session: sample point estimator, confidence-interval builder, hypothesis framer, Type II error / power analyzer, p-value vs critical-value decision maker, two-population t-tester, one-way ANOVA with Fisher's LSD, simple and multiple regression (with VIF), and regression diagnostics (quadratic fit, Cook's distance, Durbin-Watson). Each skill is applied to a business case and ends in a managerial decision. Also includes a Session 6 review that independently re-verified six hypothesis tests (Nandan Mart case).
